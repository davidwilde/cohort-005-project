# Cadence

A course platform where students buy courses and work through their lessons.

## Language

### People

**Student**:
A person who buys courses and works through their lessons.
_Avoid_: Learner, customer, buyer

**Instructor**:
A person who creates and owns courses.

**Admin**:
A person who runs the platform as a whole, above every Instructor.
_Avoid_: Team Admin (a different role)

**Staff**:
Anyone with authority over a particular course: an Admin, or the Instructor who owns that course.
_Avoid_: Admin (when only Admins are meant)

**Team Admin**:
The person who manages a team licence and its seats.
_Avoid_: Admin (a different role)

### Refunds

**Refund**:
A Student's request to get their money back for a Purchase, and the payout that follows if it is approved. A Refund is Requested, then either Approved or Declined, and only an Admin (not Staff in general) can approve or decline it. An Approved Refund ends the Student's Enrollment in that course; a Declined Refund is final and leaves the Enrollment in place.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback, money-back

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase in which a Refund can be requested, counted in whole days so the fourteenth day counts in full.
_Avoid_: Cancellation window, cancel window

**Refund Eligibility**:
The rules a Purchase must meet before a Refund can be requested: it is inside the Refund Window, has never had a Refund, has no claimed team seat, and the Student has watched less than two hours and completed less than half of the course's lessons.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer

**Refundable Amount**:
The amount a Refund gives back: always the full amount the Student actually paid for the Purchase, never more and never less.
_Avoid_: Refund price, list price, partial refund
